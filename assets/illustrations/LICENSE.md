# Illustration assets

Source: [Open Doodles](https://www.opendoodles.com) by Pablo Stanley.
License: CC0 1.0 (public domain) — free for commercial and personal use, no attribution required.

Processing: original two-color SVGs are tokenized —
`#FF5678` → `var(--illustration-accent)` and `#000000` → `var(--illustration-line)` —
so every illustration re-themes with the Flavor DS theme axes. Raw originals in `raw/`.
